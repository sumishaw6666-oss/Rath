WHO WE ARE

Supporting Families During A Difficult Time

Swargarath And Dead Body Car provides funeral transportation and related services with a focus on respectful and dignified arrangements.

Our service is designed to support families when they need dependable assistance for the final journey of their loved ones. We provide dead body car services, funeral hearse vans and other funeral-related support.

We are based in Dhapa, Kolkata, and provide local as well as long-distance transportation services.
